# Playtest analysis: two player profiles

A walkthrough of the current build (Survive The God, November 2026) from the point of view of two players: a 16-year-old and an 11-year-old. This is analysis only; nothing in the game was changed for it. It is based on Play Solo runs in Studio (desktop and the phone emulator), the round timings in `Config`, and the screens captured while testing.

Legend: ✅ works well · ⚠️ works but has friction · ❌ pain point

## The session both players go through

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Spawn in the Sky Lodge. HUD: 7 menu tiles, God Chance card (0% + "BUY · 49"), Welcome Pack offer (struck-through price), coins, DASH, quest button, Arena Cam, leaderboards, streak chest. |
| 0:04 | Daily Reward window (7 day tiles, CLAIM). |
| 0:10 | Lobby break ends; the CHOOSE A MAP window opens (9 s). |
| 0:19-0:30 | Map builds, countdown ticks. |
| 0:30 | Round: fly-by, GOD vs SURVIVORS card, first-time cinematic (about 7 s), then 10 s prep, "SCATTER!" |
| 0:47 | Disasters start; tutorial hints (dash, follow the arrows to hide). |
| ~1:55 | Round ends (85 s), or earlier when everyone is out. Round Over card (lines, highlight, next unlock, awards). |
| ~2:05 | Back in the lodge; the cycle repeats (about 2 minutes per round). |

Eliminated players wait out the rest of the round (up to about 70 s) with the Waiting card, spectate, the obby, shop or wheel.

## Profile A: 16-year-old

Plays Roblox most days, mostly on PC, sometimes on phone. Likes games with skill expression and something to show off (Natural Disaster Survival, BedWars, Doors, Evade). Plays with 1-3 friends. Reads everything quickly and skips tutorials. Sensitive to anything that feels "for little kids" or pay-to-win.

### Checklist

| # | Question | Verdict | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Understands the premise in the first 30 s | ✅ | The GOD vs SURVIVORS card plus the cinematic make "one player is the God, everyone else survives" obvious. |
| 2 | Gets to play within 60 s | ⚠️ | About 30 s of lodge + vote before the first round, then 10 s prep. Fine with friends, slow alone. |
| 3 | Tutorial respects them | ✅ | Three short, skippable hints. |
| 4 | Survivor role feels active | ⚠️ | Dash, hiding, near-miss rewards, the flare and gear help, but most of the round is running and hiding with no way to fight back. A skilled player wants more outplay tools. |
| 5 | God role feels powerful | ✅ | Big destruction, kill confirm (hitmarker, sound, streak chip), combos. Strong hook. |
| 6 | Gets to be the God soon | ⚠️ | New-player priority helps in the first 3 rounds; after that it is a weighted lottery. In a full server (8-12 players) it is roughly once every 8-12 rounds (16-24 minutes). Buying it (49 R$) is the visible shortcut, which reads as pay-to-win to some players. |
| 7 | Rounds feel fair | ✅ | Telegraphs, the cast gap, max hit, grit and regen make deaths feel earned. |
| 8 | Visual clarity | ✅ | The visibility pass helps a lot; neon maps read well. |
| 9 | Variety | ✅ | 13 maps, Chaos Twists, God kits. Strong for D1-D7. |
| 10 | Skill expression / mastery | ⚠️ | Mastery titles, leaderboards and the survival streak exist, but there's no ranked mode, no per-map best times and no "God rating". Little to grind for after about week 2. |
| 11 | Social | ⚠️ | The friend boost is good, but there are no party/invite prompts, no "play again with this God" moment and no emotes worth showing off between rounds beyond the wheel. |
| 12 | Downtime | ❌ | Dying early means up to 70 s of waiting, then 30 s of intermission (up to about 1:40 total). Spectate and haunt help a little; the obby is too short to fill it. This is the most likely reason to leave. |
| 13 | UI noise | ⚠️ | On first join: daily reward, map vote, God chance card, Welcome Pack, quest button, popups and the feed all at once. Manageable, but it looks "busy" and some of it reads as ads. |
| 14 | Monetization feel | ⚠️ | The struck-through price on the Welcome Pack and "BUY · 49" on the God card are visible all the time in the lodge. A 16-year-old will notice and may call it cash-grabby. Passes (2x coins, VIP) feel normal. |
| 15 | Performance | ✅ | Desktop holds 160+ fps with 0.14 ms server heartbeat; the busiest maps are about 1,600-2,000 parts. The Arena Cam stall at round start is fixed. |
| 16 | Would come back tomorrow (D1) | ✅ likely | Daily reward (Solar Flare trail on day 2), quests and the "next unlock" bar give reasons. |
| 17 | Would still play in a week (D7) | ⚠️ maybe | Depends on friends and how often they get to be the God. Solo vs the AI Director gets repetitive. |

### Engagement verdict (16)

Engaged for the first few sessions: the God fantasy and destruction are a strong hook, rounds are short, and the kill confirm and Round Over card feel good. The risks are **downtime after an early death**, **low God frequency in full servers**, and a **thin long-term goal** for skilled players. Estimated D1 retention is good; D7 depends on friends.

### Pain points (16)

1. ❌ **Dead time after elimination** (up to about 1:40 to the next round). The single biggest churn risk.
2. ⚠️ **God frequency in full servers**: being the God is the best part, but after the first rounds it's rare without paying.
3. ⚠️ **Survivors can't fight back**: only dodging and hiding. A skilled player plateaus quickly.
4. ⚠️ **Always-visible purchase prompts** in the lodge (Welcome Pack strike price, God card BUY) read as pushy.
5. ⚠️ **No long-term competitive goal**: no ranks, seasons or per-map records.
6. ⚠️ **Lodge HUD density on first join.**
7. ⚠️ **Solo play is against the AI Director**, which is fine but repetitive; a low-population server feels empty.

## Profile B: 11-year-old

Plays on a tablet or phone, sometimes a parent's PC. Joins from the Home page because the thumbnail looks fun. Reads slower and skips long text; responds to colour, sounds and big buttons. Easily overwhelmed by many windows. Often plays alone or with a sibling; may not have Robux (or asks a parent).

### Checklist

| # | Question | Verdict | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Understands the premise without reading | ✅ | The VS card with faces, the purple God side and the hand in the sky are very readable. The red zone demo is the best teaching moment in the game. |
| 2 | First minute is calm enough | ❌ | Daily reward window, map vote window (with a 9 s timer), God card, Welcome Pack, popups, then the fly-by, VS card, cinematic and tutorial in quick succession. Too many things asking for attention before they've done anything. |
| 3 | Can read the words | ⚠️ | Most labels are short, but words like "Escalation", "Chaos Twist", "Sudden Death", "Structural escape", "Mastery", "Kit", "Pity"-style percentages ("God Chance 0%") are hard. "RED ZONE = INCOMING DISASTER" works because of the demo. |
| 4 | Touch controls | ✅ | DASH sits above the jump button, big tiles, phone layout tested in the emulator. |
| 5 | Knows what to do as a survivor | ✅ | "Run away from red, hide inside" is learned in round 1 (tutorial arrows to a hideout). |
| 6 | Knows what to do as the God | ⚠️ | The hotbar has 6 disasters with energy costs and cooldowns; the hint says "keys 1-5" on PC. Picking and tapping works, but energy and cooldowns aren't explained visually beyond the bar. |
| 7 | Dying feels OK | ⚠️ | Ragdoll and finisher are fun; the ELIMINATED banner plus the waiting card is clear. Waiting up to 1:40 is long for an 11-year-old; they may wander to the obby (good) or leave. |
| 8 | Rewards feel exciting | ✅ | Coins rolling up, the wheel with its ratchet sound and SPINS counter, the daily reward tiles, the confetti. |
| 9 | Understands the economy | ❌ | Coins, spins, free spins, kits, cosmetics, God skins, VFX, finishers, gear, passes, products, the Welcome Pack and the streak chest. Too many currencies and systems to understand; they'll just press things. |
| 10 | Purchase pressure (parent's view) | ❌ | The God card's "BUY · 49" and "Be the GOD" and a Welcome Pack with a struck-through price are always on screen; the wheel sells spins for Robux with random rewards. Parents may see this as aggressive. The odds are shown, which helps. |
| 11 | Visuals | ✅ | Bright, colourful, chunky; the night maps are now readable. |
| 12 | Fair and not scary | ✅ | No gore; finishers are cartoony; disasters are spectacle. Some sounds (siren, screams on taunts) can be intense but are not graphic. |
| 13 | Gets to be the God | ✅ | New-player priority means they likely get a God round in the first 2-3 rounds: a big "wow" moment for this age. |
| 14 | Would come back tomorrow (D1) | ✅ likely | The day-2 Solar Flare trail is shown as "come back tomorrow"; kids respond to that. |
| 15 | Would still play in a week (D7) | ⚠️ | Likely if they keep winning things; the many systems may tire them once the novelty fades. |

### Engagement verdict (11)

Very engaged in round 1-3 (the God hand, the VS card, destruction, colourful maps, getting to be the God early). The main risks are **overload in the first minute**, **an economy too complex to understand**, **long waits after dying**, and **purchase prompts that parents may object to**. D1 retention looks good thanks to the daily reward preview.

### Pain points (11)

1. ❌ **First-minute overload**: two windows (Daily Reward, map vote) plus offers and popups before the first round. Ideally one thing at a time, with the vote doing the talking.
2. ❌ **Economy complexity**: too many currencies and item types for this age.
3. ❌ **Always-on purchase prompts and a paid random wheel**: a parent-trust and policy risk (paid random items for minors are regulated in some regions; the odds are shown, which is good).
4. ⚠️ **Hard words** in banners, the HUD and the Round Over card ("Escalation", "Structural escapes", "Chaos Twist").
5. ⚠️ **Waiting after elimination** (up to about 1:40).
6. ⚠️ **God controls are complex** (6 abilities, energy, cooldowns, kits).
7. ⚠️ **"God Chance 0%"** on the lodge card can feel discouraging when it's 0.

## Suggested next steps (not done; for discussion)

1. ~~Shorten dead time~~ **Done:** Final Rush (the clock jumps to sudden death once only a quarter of the survivors are left) and ghost bolts (the eliminated zap survivors from spectate for coins).
2. A calmer first join: hold the Daily Reward until after the first round; hide the Welcome Pack and the God BUY button until round 2 or 3.
3. Simplify the economy view for new players (one "Coins" shop tab first, the rest unlocked later) and use simpler words on banners.
4. Give survivors one active counter-play (a short-cooldown "brace" or "shield bash" against debris).
5. Long-term goals for skilled players: seasonal ranks, per-map best survival streaks, a God rating.
6. Review the paid wheel spins against Roblox's policy on paid random items for younger audiences.
